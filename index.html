The Problem

The Risk & Business Continuity division lacked structured process documentation and data governance controls. Incident tracking was manual, audit trails were inconsistent, and there was no standardised framework for aligning operational processes with strategic governance requirements.

My Approach

1. Discovery & Workshops
Facilitated requirements gathering workshops with managers and IT. Mapped stakeholders, identified pain points, and documented current-state processes.
2. Process Mapping
Produced detailed AS-IS process maps in Visio across 6 core business processes. Identified 4 automation opportunities and 12 inefficiency points.
3. Governance Documentation
Developed SOPs, audit checklists, and control frameworks. Created business rules and process definitions ensuring compliance alignment.
4. TO-BE Design & Embedding
Designed future-state processes and worked with stakeholders to embed changes across teams, supporting the transition through to implementation.

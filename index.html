Client
Dept. of Transport WA
Duration
Nov 2024 – Apr 2025
Key Outcome

The Problem

The Risk & Business Continuity division lacked structured process documentation and data governance controls. Incident tracking was manual, audit trails were inconsistent, and there was no standardised framework aligning operational processes with strategic governance requirements.

My Approach

1. Discovery & Workshops
Facilitated requirements workshops with managers and IT. Mapped stakeholders and documented current-state processes.
2. Process Mapping
Produced detailed AS-IS process maps in Visio across 6 core business processes. Identified 4 automation opportunities.
3. Governance Documentation
Developed SOPs, audit checklists, and control frameworks ensuring compliance alignment.
4. TO-BE Design & Embedding
Designed future-state processes and worked with stakeholders to embed changes through to implementation.
